---
title: inquisitive
---

My biology teacher told my parents during one of the parent-teacher meetings that I'm an inquisitive kid. None of us understood what it meant. My parents assumed it's something positive, thanks to reinforcement learning over the years (they'd only ever heard good things about me).

I rushed back home, looked up the word in my Oxford pocket dictionary only to find that it means "too interested in finding out about many different things." At that moment, I realised I proved my teacher right.
