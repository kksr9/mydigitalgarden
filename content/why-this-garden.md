title: why this garden?
---

Before the why, a little about how it started.

I was today years old when I learnt about the concept of a digital garden in a YT video this morning. I found it interesting. After 6 hours of immersing in Claude to learn using Quartz and Git from scratch, here we are!

Now, why this garden?

* My thoughts are all over the place. I've been dumping interesting links, videos, and random thoughts into WhatsApp chats and my Notes app for years. It's a mess. This is finally my brain's repo.
* I want to actually use what I learn. I consume so much interesting stuff, only to forget it and move on to the next thing. Writing here makes me pause, think, and turn what I consume into something of my own.
* A search engine for my brain. So I can revisit an interesting idea six months later instead of wondering where the hell I saw it.
* I like knowing things (and showing it a little). I have too many interests, hobbies, and skills to fit into one box. This feels like a more honest way to show who I am than a curated Instagram feed.
* This feels like social media without actually being it. The feed is by me, of me, for me and for anyone passing by.
* Writing regularly will make me articulate and creative with new words.
* I want to share what actually works. Sure, a little validation feels nice. But I'd love for someone to read about something I tried, steal the idea, and make their own life a little better.
* And hopefully, find my kind of people. If something I write makes someone think, "Wait, me too!" that's already a pretty good reason to put it out there.
