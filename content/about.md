I'm Krati Kamna Sharma, 25 as I write this. I live in India with my parents and younger brother. Love them dearly. We have dinner together every day, and I count that as a privilege.

I work from home as an analytics consultant for an American baseball league. Graduated in Architecture from a top engineering college and (almost) hold a diploma in Nutrition and Food Sciences. Funny, because growing up I wanted to be an ocean conservationist, environmentalist, nurse, doctor; anything that involved caring.

I've been an introvert pretty much all my life, except for the years in college. Was raised to be competitive and still am. Almost enjoy it. I'm extraordinarily emotional, caring, honest, and an oversharer (this website is proof enough). Big eyes, big tears. I feel guilty when I'm wrong and have a strong moral compass.

I love winters, snowfall, colours, acting, dancing, sketching, and painting. I make handmade gifts. I eat mindfully. I cook and bake rarely; my family loves my food.

I'm a hopeless romantic. I reject the concept of space. Just kidding. I like to believe I am highly self-aware, so far. I feel dumb using generic words instead of stronger, single-word alternatives to explain the intensity of my emotions.

I love decoding languages through expressions and body language in regional dramas. I understand French. I'm good at Maths, bad at Physics. Also read a lot about psychology, human behaviour, hormones, and brain chemicals.
